\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \textbf{Dioptra}, a compact ($8.097$\,M parameter) neural architecture for monocular metric depth estimation. By incorporating camera intrinsic geometry through Trivision ray positional encoding with reflection symmetry tracking, Dioptra directly grounds transformer tokens in physical 3D optical space. The Angular Residual Attention (ARA) mechanism sharpens depth boundaries by penalizing off-axis angular distortion, while decoupled scale consistency supervision enforces global metric scale without requiring heavy foundation backbones.

Evaluated on a cross-environment split of the TartanAir simulation dataset ($15$ training environments, $8,186$ held-out test frames across two unseen 3D maps, one relit at night), Dioptra Stage~1 baseline achieves an Aligned AbsRel of $0.3073$ ($\delta_1 = 51.74\%$), while Stage~2 dynamic pinhole fine-tuning advances headline performance to \textbf{$0.2901$ Aligned AbsRel} and slashes unaligned Metric AbsRel from $0.9293$ to \textbf{$0.6043$} ($35.0\%$ error reduction). Furthermore, dynamic pinhole crop augmentation empirically validates camera-intrinsic equivariance, reducing telephoto error by up to \textbf{$55.9\%$} across multi-camera field-of-view sweeps ($50^\circ \text{--} 100^\circ$). Cross-domain testing on 40 uncompressed ground-truth held-out arrays demonstrates resilient out-of-distribution transfer under daylight industrial geometry ($0.2290$ AbsRel, $86.6\%$ $\delta_2$), low-light night conditions ($0.3291$–$0.5195$ AbsRel), and large-scale outdoor geometry ($0.6239$ domain mean with best-frame reaching $0.2663$), yielding an overall held-out mean of $0.5545$ AbsRel ($0.4577$ robust mean) for the Stage~1 baseline, with Stage~2 fine-tuning achieving $\mathbf{0.7420}$ Metric AbsRel. Operating within $180$\,MB peak RAM with $52.8$\,ms steady-state FP32 forward inference via layer-shared geometric caching ($\approx 18.9$\,FPS; $98.4$\,ms unoptimized reference; $28.2$\,ms on NVIDIA T4; $334.9$\,ms full 3D mesh pipeline), Dioptra demonstrates that camera-grounded geometric inductive biases enable viable metric depth estimation within an edge-compatible computational envelope.
