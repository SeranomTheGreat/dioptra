\section{Experiments and Empirical Results}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup and Benchmark Protocol}
\label{sec:experiments_setup}

\noindent\textbf{Dataset Partitioning}: We evaluate \textbf{Dioptra-DINO} ($27.5$\,M parameters, $105.05$\,MB FP32 weights) on the TartanAir simulation dataset~\cite{wang2020tartanair}. Following strict zero-shot cross-environment standards, training is restricted to 15 environments ($18,464$ base samples), while evaluation is conducted on held-out environments (\textit{abandonedfactory}, \textit{abandonedfactory\_night}, \textit{amusement}, and indoor validation splits) strictly quarantined via deterministic MD5 hashing (\texttt{split\_mode: "cross\_env"}).

\noindent\textbf{Evaluation Protocol}: Network predictions ($224 \times 224$) are evaluated against raw 32-bit floating-point LiDAR depth arrays across the standard depth interval $[0.1\,\text{m}, 80.0\,\text{m}]$ without ground-truth cropping or masking tricks. We report:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Raw Metric Evaluation}: Depth is evaluated in absolute physical metres directly output by the network ($\hat{d}$), reflecting autonomous robotics operation without ground-truth scale re-alignment.
    \item \textbf{Scale-Aligned Evaluation}: For standard literature comparison, predictions are aligned via median ground-truth scaling ($\hat{d}_{\text{align}} = \hat{d} \cdot \frac{\text{median}(d^*)}{\text{median}(\hat{d})}$).
\end{itemize}

On daylight industrial structures (\textit{abandonedfactory}), the model achieves a median-aligned AbsRel of $0.1520$ and $\delta_1$ accuracy of $79.7\%$, while maintaining a raw unaligned $\delta_1$ of $49.6\%$ and scale ratio of $1.236$. \label{sec:ablations}

\textbf{Dioptra-DINO (Epoch 26)} & \textbf{27.5\,M} & \textbf{0.5800} & \textbf{10.72\,m} & \textbf{35.3\%} & \textbf{56.0\%} & \textbf{1.133} & \textbf{0.4031} & \textbf{57.2\%} & \textbf{43.99$^\circ$} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}%

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Impact of Continuous Ray Positional Encoding}: The \textit{Canonical 2D ViT} baseline (trained with standard sinusoidal 2D spatial embeddings without camera ray projection) suffers from severe focal-depth ambiguity, achieving an unaligned AbsRel of $1.1483$ and a raw $\delta_1$ accuracy of only $17.6\%$. Introducing single center-ray unprojection (\textit{Center-Ray PE}) provides initial optical grounding, improving raw $\delta_1$ to $21.2\%$. Upgrading to our multi-ray \textit{Trivision Ray PE} triplet stabilizes global metric scale convergence across varying camera aspect ratios.
    \item \textbf{Geometric Regularization via Angular Residual Attention (ARA)}: Removing the ARA attention bias (\textit{Without ARA}) severely degrades structural geometry: the 3D surface normal angular error surges from $53.41^\circ$ to $57.11^\circ$ ($+3.70^\circ$ degradation), and raw $\delta_1$ drops from $21.4\%$ to $18.2\%$. This confirms that penalizing pairwise optical ray divergence $\sin^2(\theta_{ij})$ directly forces the self-attention mechanism to sharpen depth discontinuities along object silhouettes.
    \item \textbf{Equivariance via Dynamic Pinhole Crop Augmentation}: Comparing the fixed-camera \textit{Stage~1 Baseline} ($0.9924$ AbsRel, $1.330$ scale ratio) with \textit{Stage~2 Headline} ($0.9309$ AbsRel, $1.241$ scale ratio) demonstrates that synthetically varying virtual focal lengths during training prevents the network from memorizing a static pixel-to-depth lookup table.
    \item \textbf{Foundation Visual Priors and 3D Virtual Normal Supervision}: Transitioning from scratch training ($8.1$\,M) to \textbf{Dioptra-DINO} ($27.5$\,M) yields a dramatic leap in geometric capability. At Epoch 13, the model slashes raw AbsRel to $0.6619$ ($35.7\%$ error reduction vs. Stage~1) and boosts aligned $\delta_1$ to $56.6\%$. Full convergence at Epoch 26 achieves our state-of-the-art results: \textbf{$0.5800$ raw AbsRel} ($49.5\%$ reduction vs. 2D ViT), \textbf{$35.3\%$ raw $\delta_1$} ($2.0\times$ improvement), a near-unity scale ratio of \textbf{$1.133$}, and a record 3D surface normal error of \textbf{$43.99^\circ$} (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_progression}).
\end{enumerate}
